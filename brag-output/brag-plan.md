# Brag Plan: ReliefLens

## What is this app?
ReliefLens is an emergency rescue coordination platform that turns chaotic disaster data into clear, explainable rescue decisions with experienced human rescue chiefs always in control.

## The angle
A high-stakes, cinematic trailer: When catastrophic floodwaters breach a city and communications overflow, computers calculate danger with mathematical transparency, but real human chiefs always review the evidence and deploy the boats.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dramatic cinematic letterbox opening over a pitch-black satellite radar grid. A pulsing crimson vector beacon explodes across the screen: "ACTIVE DISASTER RESPONSE ZONE // WATER RISING 5 INCHES/HOUR". 
Voiceover opens with authority: *"When floodwaters rise... every single second is a life."*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Live Situation Grid (4.5s – 9.5s):** The interactive radar beam sweeps across the flood basin. Pins lock onto 18 active emergencies, highlighting St. Jude Senior Care where 45 residents are trapped by 6-foot water.
- **Explainable Prioritization Formula (9.5s – 14.5s):** The math decomposes live on screen: `40%(DANGER) + 30%(PEOPLE IN DANGER) + 20%(TIME LEFT) + 10%(LOCATION CHECK) = 88/100 CRITICAL RESCUE`. Zero black-box secrets.
- **Failsafe Dispatch & Chief Sign-Off (14.5s – 17.5s):** The safety slider illuminates. The Chief slides to confirm: *"AQUATIC RESCUE BOAT R-3 DISPATCHED — ETA 8 MIN"*.

## Outro / punchline
Monumental brand typography slams into view with glowing bio-yellow light:
**"RELIEFLENS — When disasters move fast, help moves faster."**
Subtext: *Free Public Emergency Service · Human-in-the-Loop Rescue Coordination.*

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Citizen reports rising water at 42 River Road.
2. **Key Action:** Deduplication engine links the call to 3 neighbors on the same street, updating location confidence to 98%.
3. **Result:** Rescue Commander slides to deploy Rescue Boat R-3; permanent cryptographic ledger records the signed justification.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Breach (0.0s – 4.5s)
- **Visuals:** Full-bleed dark grid. Crimson hazard beacons pulse across coordinates. Flood water surge vector animates across the screen (+5.5 inches/hour). Letterbox cinema bars frame the view.
- **Audio:** Low sub-bass rumble, distant thunder, sonar pulse, and Voiceover: *"When catastrophic floodwaters rise... every single second is a life."*
- **Transition:** Quick cinematic zoom into tactical command center.

### Scene 2 — The Situation Room (4.5s – 9.5s)
- **Visuals:** The live 60/40 ReliefLens interface appears. The radar beam sweeps across the flooded river basin. Markers lock onto `#FL-8042 (St. Jude Senior Care, 45 Trapped)`. Nearby Rescue Boat R-3 and Helicopter SAR-1 illuminate with real-time telemetry.
- **Audio:** Radar sweep hum, fast tactical telemetry clicks, Voiceover: *"ReliefLens maps the crisis in real time, finding trapped families and routing rescue crews."*
- **Transition:** Smooth camera push-in into the Explainable Scorecard.

### Scene 3 — The Transparent Decision (9.5s – 14.5s)
- **Visuals:** Chamfered scorecard card expands. The 4 common-sense vectors count up dynamically with live bar meters: Danger (95) + People (82) + Time (90) + Location (42) = 88/100 CRITICAL. A badge confirms: *"100% HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP · NO BLACK BOX SECRETS"*.
- **Audio:** High-speed mechanical counter ticks, affirmative chime, Voiceover: *"Every decision is transparent. Every score is verified. And real human chiefs make the final call."*
- **Transition:** Cut to dispatch action HUD.

### Scene 4 — Deploy & Monolith Outro (14.5s – 20.0s)
- **Visuals:** The red safety slider snaps across: `SLIDE RIGHT TO SEND RESCUE ➔` → `✓ RESCUE BOAT R-3 DISPATCHED`. Screen cuts to monumental outline typography: `RELIEFLENS`, glowing in bio-yellow with subtitle: *"When disasters move fast, help moves faster."*
- **Audio:** Heavy hydraulic slide latch lock, rescue siren chirp, triumphant music swell, Voiceover: *"ReliefLens. Because when disasters move fast, help moves faster."*
- **End frame:** Settled high-contrast logo frame suitable for thumbnail/poster.
