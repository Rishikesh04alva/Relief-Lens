How ReliefLens gets help to people faster, fairly, and without secrets.

01 · Clear Reasons, No Secrets

When you call 911 in a flood, you deserve to know you are being taken seriously. ReliefLens breaks down danger using 4 clear facts anyone can understand: How deep is the water? Are children or seniors trapped? How quickly is the road cutting off? Nothing is hidden behind confusing jargon.

02 · Real Humans Make the Final Decision

No computer program should make life-or-death decisions alone. Software does the math to recommend who needs help fastest, but experienced rescue chiefs review every single situation and sign their name before deploying rescue boats, helicopters, or ambulances.

RESCUE CHIEF ON DUTY: CAPTAIN MARCUS VANCE
✓ SIGNED DECISION RECORDED

HUMAN OVERSIGHT PROTECTS EVERY COMMUNITY

03 · Grouping Repeated Calls on the Same Street

When a whole street floods, 40 neighbors might all call for help at the same time. ReliefLens automatically groups calls from the same location together. This ensures rescue dispatchers don't get overwhelmed and prevents sending five boats to one house while another neighborhood is left waiting.
